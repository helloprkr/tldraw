9. **[untyped]** Attention is not spent. It is fed. `009-fed-not-spent`
10. **[untyped]** So I no longer ask how to protect my attention, as though guarding a balance, but what I am asking it to metabolize, and whether the diet I am feeding it—the feeds, the fragments, the endless untethered paragraphs—is a diet a practice can hold, and tend, rather than merely survive. `010-the-stance`
11. **[untyped]** Under the spending view, rest is the only cure, and so we are told to unplug, to detox, to abstain—language borrowed wholesale from addiction, as though the mind were an account to be left alone until it refilled itself. `011-borrowed-from-addiction`
12. **[untyped]** Under the metabolic view, rest still matters, but so does what gets carried in: the discipline is not withdrawal but composition, the slow work of choosing a diet of claims and figures and long clausal sentences the mind can actually break down, hold, and eventually turn into something of its own—which is, I think, the only definition of understanding I still trust, and the only reason I keep writing sentences this long instead of shorter ones that would spend less and carry less as well. `012-a-diet-a-practice-can-hold`
