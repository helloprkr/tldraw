Under the metabolic view, rest still matters, but so does what gets carried in: the discipline is not withdrawal but composition, the slow work of choosing a diet of claims and figures and long clausal sentences the mind can actually break down, hold, and eventually turn into something of its own—which is, I think, the only definition of understanding I still trust, and the only reason I keep writing sentences this long instead of shorter ones that would spend less and carry less as well.
